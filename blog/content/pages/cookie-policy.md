## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They let the site, or third parties working with it, recognize your browser on later visits. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels and local storage, and we refer to all of them as "cookies" in this policy.

## Cookies we use

**Strictly necessary.** Our Site itself does not set cookies to work. Our hosting provider or content delivery network may set short-lived technical cookies for security, load balancing and protection against abuse. These cannot be switched off because the Site would not work safely without them.

**Consent cookies.** When you make a choice in our consent message, a cookie stores that choice so we do not ask again on every page. This message is provided by Google, a Google-certified consent management platform.

**Advertising cookies.** We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to:

- show ads and limit how often you see the same ad;
- measure ad performance and detect fraud;
- personalize ads based on your interests and your visits to this and other websites, where you have consented or where the law allows it.

Examples include Google cookies such as `__gads`, `__gpi`, `IDE` and `DSID`. The exact cookies and their lifetimes are set by Google and may change; Google explains them in [How Google uses cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies) and [Advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

**Affiliate cookies.** If you click an affiliate link to a retailer, that retailer may set a cookie to record that you came from our Site. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Your choices

- **Consent message.** Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message on their first visit. You can accept, reject, or choose which purposes and vendors to allow.
- **Change your mind at any time.** Use the **"Privacy & cookie settings"** link in the footer of every page to reopen the consent message and update or withdraw your consent.
- **US residents** can use the same link, where shown, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.
- **Ad personalization.** You can turn off personalized ads from Google at [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/), and from many other ad companies at [www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or [www.youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).
- **Browser settings.** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. If you block all cookies, some features may not work and you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
