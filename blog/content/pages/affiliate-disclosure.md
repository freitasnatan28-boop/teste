{{site.name}} is free to read. It is paid for in two ways.

**Advertising.** We show ads served by Google AdSense. We do not choose the individual ads, and an ad is not an endorsement.

**Affiliate links.** Some guides may link to hotels, tours, travel insurance or booking sites. If you book through one of these links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

## What this means for our guides

- No company pays to appear in our rankings or to change what we write about it.
- We recommend a booking site or service only when we think it is genuinely useful for the trip we are describing.
- Affiliate links are marked with `rel="sponsored"` in our code, as Google requires, and guides that contain them say so near the top.
