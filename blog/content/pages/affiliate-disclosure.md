{{site.name}} is free to read. To cover the costs of running it, we earn money in two ways:

1. **Advertising.** We show ads served by Google AdSense. We do not choose the individual ads you see, and an ad appearing on the Site is not an endorsement.
2. **Affiliate links.** Some articles may contain links to products at online retailers. If you click one of these links and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

## What this means for our recommendations

- Most of our projects are built from free materials you already have, and we will always tell you when a homemade option is as good as, or better than, buying something.
- We only recommend products we believe are genuinely useful for our readers' pets. A commission never decides whether a product is included or how it is described.
- When we have personally used a product, we say so. When we have not, we say that too.
- Affiliate links are marked with `rel="sponsored"` in our code, as required by Google, and articles that contain them include a short notice.
