---
title: Contact
description: How to contact Carimbo with corrections, questions or partnership requests.
---

The quickest way to reach us is by email:

**[{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}})**

We usually reply within two or three working days.

**Corrections.** If something in a guide is out of date (a closed attraction, a changed price, a new entry rule), please tell us which guide and what changed. We check every report and update the guide.

**Questions.** We are happy to answer questions about the places we cover, especially Brazil. We cannot book trips or give legal advice about visas; for that, contact the relevant embassy or consulate.

**Partnerships.** Tourism boards, hotels and brands can write to the same address. Sponsored content, if we ever publish it, will always be labelled.

For how we handle the information you send us, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).
