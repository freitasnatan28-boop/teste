We read every message. The fastest way to reach us is by email:

**[{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}})**

We usually reply within two to three business days.

## What to write to us about

- **Questions about a project.** Tell us which article you are building and where you got stuck. A photo helps.
- **Corrections and safety concerns.** If you think something in an article is wrong or unsafe, please tell us. We review every report and update the article when needed.
- **Your builds.** We love seeing what you made. If you send a photo, let us know whether we may share it on the site and how you would like to be credited.
- **Partnerships.** Brands and publishers can write to the same address. We only work with products we would use with our own pets, and sponsored content is always labeled.

## Please note

We cannot answer medical questions about your pet. If your pet is sick, injured, or has swallowed something it should not have, contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.

For questions about how we handle personal data, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).
