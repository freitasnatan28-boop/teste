Ask "when is the best time to visit Brazil?" and the honest answer is "where in Brazil?". The country crosses the equator and runs south almost to the latitude of Buenos Aires. While the South is cold and grey in July, the Northeast is sunny, and the Pantanal is at its best for wildlife.

Seasons are also the reverse of Europe and North America. Summer runs from December to March, and winter from June to September.

## Rio de Janeiro and the Southeast

Summer, from December to March, is hot and humid, with heavy afternoon storms. It is also high season, with Christmas, New Year and Carnival all falling in it. The beaches are at their liveliest and prices are at their highest.

Our favourite months are April to June and August to October. The air is drier, the sky is clearer from the viewpoints, and it is still warm enough for the beach most days. Winter (June to August) is mild by most standards, but evenings can be cool and the sea is cold.

## The Northeast: Salvador, Recife, Fortaleza

The Northeast is warm all year, and the main question is rain. On the coast around Salvador and Recife, the wettest months are roughly April to July. North of there, around Fortaleza and the Lençóis Maranhenses, the rainy season comes earlier, in the first half of the year.

For beaches, September to March is usually the most reliable. For the Lençóis Maranhenses, go from June to September, when the lagoons between the dunes are full.

## The Amazon

The Amazon has a wetter season (roughly December to May) and a drier season (roughly June to November), but it rains at any time of year.

High water, around May and June, floods the forest, and canoes can paddle between the trees. Low water, around October and November, opens up beaches along the rivers and forest trails. Both are good; they are simply different trips.

## The Pantanal

Go in the dry season, from about July to October. As the floodwaters retreat, animals gather around the remaining rivers and lagoons, and they are much easier to see. It is also the main season for jaguar trips in the north. In the wet season, many roads and some lodges close.

## Iguaçu Falls

The falls are impressive all year. The volume of water is usually highest in the rainy summer months, and the spray is heavier. In winter, the paths are less crowded and the weather is cooler for walking.

## The South: Florianópolis and the Serra Gaúcha

The South has four real seasons. December to March is beach season. In winter, temperatures in the mountains of the Serra Gaúcha can drop close to freezing, which is part of the charm of towns like Gramado. If you want the sea, avoid June to August.

## Carnival, holidays and prices

Carnival happens 47 days before Easter Sunday, so the date moves every year. In 2027, it falls in early February. The parades and street parties are extraordinary, but flights and hotels in Rio, Salvador and Recife cost several times more and sell out early. Book months ahead, or avoid those cities that week if you are not going for Carnival.

Other expensive periods are Christmas and New Year, when the whole country goes to the coast, and the July school holidays.

## A quick month-by-month summary

| Months | Good for | Think twice about |
|---|---|---|
| December to March | Beaches in the South and Southeast, Carnival | Heat and storms in Rio, wet season in the Pantanal and Amazon |
| April to June | Rio and the Southeast, fewer crowds, Amazon high water | Rain on the Salvador and Recife coast |
| July to September | Pantanal wildlife, Lençóis Maranhenses, Northeast beaches start | Cold sea in the South, July school holidays |
| October to November | Pantanal, Amazon low water, Rio before the heat | The start of the rainy season in central Brazil |

## Our short answer

If you can only choose one window for a first trip that combines Rio with somewhere else, aim for **late August to October**: dry in the Pantanal, pleasant in Rio, good in the Northeast and outside the main holidays.
