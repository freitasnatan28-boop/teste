Hawaii was an independent kingdom with a queen, a palace with electric lights and treaties with the great powers. Then a group of American businessmen, backed by US Marines, took it over. Here is how sugar, a naval base and one night in 1893 turned a kingdom into the 50th state, and why Congress apologized a century later.
