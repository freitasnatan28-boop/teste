In 1946, the US secretly offered Denmark $100 million in GOLD for Greenland. Denmark said no, and the offer stayed hidden until 1991.

But buying land is exactly how America was built. Napoleon sold Louisiana for 3 cents an acre. Russia sold Alaska for 2 cents an acre. Denmark sold the Virgin Islands for $25 million in gold. Here is every deal behind the map, and why Greenland breaks the pattern.
