In 1999, America handed the Panama Canal to Panama. Panama paid $0.

America took a piece of Colombia, built the greatest shortcut on Earth, ran it like a little America for 85 years, and then handed it over for nothing. Now Washington wants it back. Here is the whole story behind the map: the French disaster, the revolution Teddy Roosevelt backed, the flag riots of 1964, the treaty that passed by one vote, and why the canal matters more than ever.
